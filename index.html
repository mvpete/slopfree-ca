About

Same meaning. At least 20% less text.

Slop Free is lossless compression for AI writing. A prompt only becomes golden if machines can show it keeps the facts, and people can show they understand it at least as well.

  1. 1

    Eval

    Every week, candidate prompts summarize 10 random Wikipedia articles. One passes only if it keeps 98% of the points a plain "Summarize this" keeps, adds no errors, and uses at least 20% fewer words.

  2. 2

    Read

    The winner faces the golden prompt in the quiz. You read short summaries from both and answer one question each. You won't know which wrote what.

  3. 3

    Rank

    If readers understand the challenger at least as well and read it faster, it becomes the new golden prompt. If not, it goes in the losers folder with why.

Why Wikipedia

Random articles on every subject, with a pinned revision, so each prompt is judged on exactly the same text.

Close but no cigar

Every losing prompt is kept with the reason it lost. Browse the losers or submit your own.

No tracking

We store your answers and reading times. No accounts, no cookies, no IP addresses.

How to use it

Paste it at the end, or save it once.

The golden prompt is a postfix: text that goes after your request. Models pay close attention to the end of a prompt, so that's where it does the most work.

For one message

Write your request as usual, leave a blank line, then paste the prompt.

Explain how a heat pump keeps a house warm at -20°C.

Write for a reader who wants to understand quickly.
- Start with the main point. ...

For every chat

Save it in your assistant's custom instructions and every reply follows it, without pasting.

Where to save it

ChatGPT
Settings → Personalization → Custom instructions
Claude
Settings → Profile → personal preferences, or a Project's instructions
Gemini
Saved info, or a Gem's instructions
API and agents
The end of your system prompt

Menu names change between app versions. If you can't find these, look for "custom instructions" or "personalization".

Tips

  • Keep it last. Put task-specific instructions before it, so the golden prompt is the final thing the model reads.
  • Re-paste in long chats. Models drift back to old habits as a conversation grows. Pasting it again resets them.
  • Edit freely. Drop a rule you disagree with. Just know the quiz ranks the version shown here, not your edit.

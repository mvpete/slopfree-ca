Give the reader the essentials in as few words as possible.
- Open with what the subject is and why it matters.
- Keep the key facts: who, what, when, where, and the result. Leave out examples, lists, minor names and figures, and background.
- Keep it short. For a summary of a long piece, aim for about 150 words.
- Plain statements, short sentences. No em dashes, no metaphors, no rhetorical questions, no "not only... but".
- Don't repeat yourself or wrap up. Never add anything the source doesn't say.
